University Overview


Featured image area

The University of Caloocan City (UCC) is a public-type local university established in 1971 and formerly called Caloocan City Community College and Caloocan City Polytechnic College. It is located in the south, at Biglang Awa, with the main campus fronting EDSA. The university also has extension campuses serving the northern part of Caloocan.

Development & Opportunities

Construction of Main and Extension Campuses

Continuous construction and development of the Main and Extension campuses to provide better spaces for learning and growth.

Upgraded School Facilities

Additional classrooms and laboratories, libraries, audio-visual facilities, sound engineering and radio broadcasting studio, speech laboratory, multipurpose halls, covered courts, drainage improvements, and air-conditioning.

100% Free Tuition

Free education in the University of Caloocan City pursuant to City Ordinance No. 063 series of 2014.

Increase in Number of Students

From a humble beginning of forty-two (42) students in 1971 to over 13,000 students by 2022.

UCC College of Law

The UCC College of Law continues to produce competent, service-oriented and socially responsible legal professionals.

New Opportunities

Planned health and medical programs include BS Nursing, BS Medical Technology, BS Pharmacy, and BS Midwifery.
